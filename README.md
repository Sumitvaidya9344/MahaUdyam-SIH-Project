# MahaUdyam – Unified Industrial Approval & Compliance Platform

**MahaUdyam** is an intelligent, unified platform designed to simplify and streamline the process of industrial approvals, registrations, licenses, NOCs, inspections, compliance, renewals, and access to government support services.

The platform provides entrepreneurs with a **personalized approval roadmap** based on their business type, industry, location, project size, and operational stage.

## 🚀 Key Features

* 🏢 **Business Registration & Profile Management**
* 📋 **Personalized Approval Checklist**
* 📄 **Digital Document Management**
* 🤖 **Document Pre-Validation**
* 📊 **Application Status Tracking**
* 🔍 **Department Scrutiny & Query Management**
* 🏗️ **Inspection Scheduling & Tracking**
* ⏱️ **SLA & Delay Monitoring**
* 🔄 **Compliance & Renewal Management**
* 💰 **Government Schemes & Incentive Discovery**
* 📨 **Grievance Management**
* 🔔 **Notifications & Alerts**
* 📈 **Department & Admin Analytics Dashboard**

## 🎯 Problem

Entrepreneurs and industrial units often need to obtain multiple approvals, registrations, licenses, permissions, NOCs, inspections, and renewals from different authorities. Requirements can vary according to sector, location, project size, and stage of operation.

MahaUdyam aims to provide a **single digital platform** that makes this entire journey easier, more transparent, and easier to track.

## 💡 Solution

MahaUdyam connects **entrepreneurs and government departments** through a centralized workflow.

```text

Personalized Approval Checklist
       ↓

       ↓

       ↓

```

## 🔄 Application Workflow

1. Entrepreneur registers on the platform.
2. Business and project details are entered.
3. The system generates a customized approval checklist.
4. Required documents are uploaded.
5. Documents are pre-validated.
6. Application is submitted to the relevant department.
7. Department officer reviews the application.
8. Queries or corrections are communicated digitally.
9. Inspection is scheduled when required.
10. Application is approved after successful scrutiny.
11. Compliance and renewal deadlines are monitored.
12. Relevant government schemes and incentives are displayed.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of MahaUdyam is to create a **single-window digital experience** for industrial approvals and compliance, connecting applicants and government departments through an intelligent and transparent workflow.

## 🏛️ Problem Statement

**Problem Statement ID:** 26130

**Title:** Efficiency in streamlining industrial approvals, compliance processes, and access to government support services

**Organization:** Government of Maharashtra

**Department:** Maharashtra State Innovation Society, Department of Skills, Employment, Entrepreneurship and Innovation

**Category:** Software

The project addresses the requirement for a unified solution capable of generating customized approval checklists, guiding documentation, tracking applications, coordinating workflows, scheduling inspections, monitoring timelines, managing renewals, and providing access to incentives and support schemes.
